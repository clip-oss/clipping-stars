Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ClippingStars, the short-form video clipping agency with 150+ editors and 4.6 billion views generated.

About ClippingStars

What is ClippingStars?

+

ClippingStars is a short-form video clipping agency that converts long-form content into viral short-form clips for platforms like TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. ClippingStars has a team of over 150 editors worldwide and has generated more than 4.6 billion views for its clients, including top YouTubers, podcasters, and content creators.

How many editors does ClippingStars have?

+

ClippingStars has a global team of over 150 editors, clippers, and content strategists operating across four time zones including the US, UK, Australia, and UAE.

What makes ClippingStars different from other clipping agencies?

+

ClippingStars assigns a dedicated team of clippers, editors, and strategists to each client rather than using shared resources. The agency also operates across four time zones to ensure content is posted at peak hours in every region, without using VPNs or fake accounts.

How ClippingStars Works

How does ClippingStars work?

+

ClippingStars follows a four-step process: you apply through the website, then join a strategy call to discuss your content goals, after which a dedicated team of clippers, editors, and content strategists takes over your short-form content creation and distribution across TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

What do I need to provide to ClippingStars?

+

You only need to provide ClippingStars with access to your long-form content. The ClippingStars team handles everything else, including clipping, editing, adding captions, content strategy, and posting across all platforms.

Does ClippingStars handle content strategy?

+

Yes, ClippingStars provides full content strategy in addition to clipping and editing. The team handles ideation, A/B testing, analytics tracking, and weekly performance reports to continuously optimize your short-form content performance.

What types of content does ClippingStars work with?

+

ClippingStars works with any long-form video content including YouTube videos, podcast episodes, livestreams, interviews, and educational content. The team transforms this content into engaging short-form clips optimized for each platform.
